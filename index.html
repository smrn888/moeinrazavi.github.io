Profile

I am a mechanical engineer with an M.Sc. in Control Systems from Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST). My interests lie in robotics, nonlinear and fault-tolerant control, model predictive control (MPC), and reinforcement learning for autonomous systems.

My projects span simulated CMG motorcycle stabilization, fault-tolerant UAV control, dual-arm KUKA manipulation in Drake, and an Industry 4.0 pick-and-place pipeline integrating CODESYS PLC, OPC UA, computer vision, trajectory planning, and PyBullet. I am seeking PhD opportunities in robotics, autonomous systems, and control.
