Profile

Mechanical engineer with an M.Sc. in control systems from Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST). My research focuses on autonomous systems and robotics, in particular fault-tolerant control for UAVs and for motorcycles stabilized by control moment gyroscopes (CMGs).

I write the software that makes the theory run: convex MPC solvers, reinforcement learning agents, and ROS 2 / Gazebo simulations. I am currently applying for PhD positions.
